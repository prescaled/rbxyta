# Guests

Guests are the heart of every shift: they create the [[Shift Tasks]], they pay out (through the [[End of Shift Inspection]]), and some of them are not what they seem ([[Incidents]]).

## How guests arrive (decided 2026-10-24)
- **A different number of guests checks in every night.** Some nights are quiet, some are busy.
- **Guests who are well looked after stay for a few nights.** A happy guest extends their stay, so the hotel fills up over a run of shifts.
- **Some guests arrive as a group and share one room** (couples, families, friends, work crews). One room, several sets of needs.

## Guest stay cycle
1. **Arrive** at reception, alone or as a group.
2. **Check in**: a player assigns a room and hands over a key card.
3. **Requests during the night**: towels, room service, a fix, a wake-up call, a complaint.
4. **Satisfaction** goes up when requests are handled fast and well, down when ignored.
5. **Decide**: a satisfied guest stays another night; an unhappy one checks out early.
6. **Check out** at the end of the stay, which frees the room for cleaning.

## Proposed rules (not final, tune in playtests)
| Rule | Starting value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New guests per night | Random 3 to 8 (scales with player count) | Busier nights later in a run |
| Group size | 1 to 4 per room | Groups ask for more, pay more |
| Stay length | 1 night, +1 per night they end happy, max 4 | Long stays build pressure on the team |
| Satisfaction | 0 to 100 per room | Feeds the inspection grade |

## Guest types (ideas)
- **Regular**: easy, polite, a few requests.
- **Demanding**: many requests, big tip if pleased.
- **Group**: shares a room, requests stack up.
- **Night owl**: calls at odd hours.
- **Odd guest**: looks normal but is part of the mystery (wrong date on their ID, no record of arrival, asks for a room that does not exist). Links to [[Missing Guests]] and [[Room 0]].

## Open
