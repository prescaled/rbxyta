# Gameplay Loop

One shift = one session. The first version should focus on **one excellent gameplay loop** ([[Principles]]).

## The loop
1. **Clock in** as a [[Night Shift Employees|night-shift employee]], alone or with friends.
2. **Work the shift**: [[Shift Tasks]] such as check-ins, deliveries, maintenance, cameras and room calls.
3. **Something strange happens**: an [[Incidents|incident]] interrupts the routine.
4. **Investigate while still working**: decide whether to follow the oddity or keep up with the job.
5. **Collect evidence**: documents, records, objects, camera clips.
6. **Finish the shift** at morning.
7. **Progress**: unlock restricted areas and records ([[Progression]]), then start a new shift where things are slightly different.

## Key rules
- The objective is not just to survive until morning. It is to complete the shift **and** uncover evidence.
- Every shift has different incidents, clues and decisions.
- Some events are harmless, others lead deeper into the hotel's history ([[Player Experience#Uncertainty]]).

## Design questions
- How long is a shift in real time (10, 15, 20 minutes)?
- What happens if tasks are ignored? Is there a work-performance score?
- See [[Open Questions]].
