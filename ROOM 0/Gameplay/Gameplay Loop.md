# Gameplay Loop

One shift = one session. The first version should focus on **one excellent gameplay loop** ([[Principles]]).

## The loop
1. **Clock in** as a [[Night Shift Employees|night-shift employee]], alone or with friends.
2. **Guests arrive**: a different number every night, some in groups sharing one room, some still staying from earlier nights ([[Guests]]).
3. **Work the shift**: [[Shift Tasks]] such as check-ins, deliveries, cleaning, maintenance, cameras and room calls. Random bonus rewards can pop up for certain actions ([[Variable Rewards]]).
4. **Shop window**: the [[Hotel Shop]] opens for only part of the shift. Spend coins on small boosts, or reroll its stock for Robux.
5. **Something strange happens**: [[Incidents]] and [[Strange Events]] interrupt the routine, and get stranger as the run goes on.
6. **Investigate while still working**: decide whether to follow the oddity or keep up with the job.
7. **Collect evidence**: documents, records, objects, camera clips.
8. **Finish the shift** at morning.
9. **Inspection**: [[The Inspector]] checks the hotel and pays coins by grade ([[End of Shift Inspection]]).
10. **Progress**: happy guests stay more nights, players unlock restricted areas and records ([[Progression]]), then a new shift starts.

## Key rules
- The objective is not just to survive until morning. It is to complete the shift **and** uncover evidence.
- Every shift has different incidents, clues and decisions.
- Some events are harmless, others lead deeper into the hotel's history ([[Player Experience#Uncertainty]]).

## Design questions
- How long is a shift in real time (10, 15, 20 minutes)?
- What happens if tasks are ignored? Answer: guests get unhappy, check out early, and the inspection grade (and coins) drop.
- See [[Open Questions]].
