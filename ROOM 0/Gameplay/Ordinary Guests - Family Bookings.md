# Ordinary Guests - Family Bookings

**Requested by the user on 2026-10-26:** some guests come with friends, and some come **with kids**. Friends are already covered by [[Ordinary Guests - Group Bookings]] (T01, Q02). These three bookings add children, with the room arrangements and supervision rules [[Ordinary Guests - Group Bookings]] said a family booking would need. Names, ages and details are **proposals**. Looks are in [[Ordinary Guests - Character Looks]].

## Rules for children (all family bookings)

- **Children are ordinary guests, always.** They are never anomalies, never predators' targets, never part of the missing cases, and never hurt, chased or frightened on screen. A [[Strange Events|strange event]] near a child happens to the adults or the hotel, not to the child. When the hotel turns dangerous later in a run, families have already checked out or are asleep behind a locked door.
- **Always with their grown-up.** A child moves with a parent or grandparent. They may step a few studs away (to look at the palm or the notice board), but they never walk the corridors alone, and never at night.
- **Requests come through the adult.** A child can *prompt* a request ("Dad, I'm cold"), but the adult asks the staff.
- **Check-in:** the booking lists every child by name and age. Children don't have ID cards; the adult's card and the booking cover them. A child is never a reason to refuse a booking.
- **Rooms:** every person needs their own sleeping space (a cot does not exist yet). Kids sleep in single beds or share a double only with their parent.

## F01 · Grace Okafor, 38; Samuel Okafor, 41; Zara Okafor, 9; Kofi Okafor, 6 · The family on the long drive

**Visit:** one night, breaking a long drive to the children's grandparents. **Room:** Family room (106: four singles).

**Personalities:** Grace plans everything and has a snack for every situation. Samuel is calm, slow and good at carrying three bags at once. Zara reads every sign in the hotel out loud. Kofi is tired, sticky and attached to a toy rabbit.

**Arrival:** Kofi is half asleep on Samuel's shoulder. Zara asks whether the hotel has a swimming pool (it doesn't) and accepts the lounge instead.

**Routine:** settle, a quick snack in the lounge, the children asleep early, the parents quietly reading. Up early to drive on.

**Request choices:** an extra pillow for Kofi; a spare blanket; a wake-up call. **Small scene:** Kofi leaves his rabbit in the lounge; it is found and returned to the room (an ordinary lost-property moment, not a mystery).

## F02 · Lucy Brennan, 34; Alfie Brennan, 7 · Mum and son, football weekend

**Visit:** two nights for Alfie's junior football tournament nearby. **Room:** Twin (104).

**Personalities:** Lucy is cheerful, a little frazzled, and always on the phone to the team's group chat. Alfie is football-mad, never still, and polite when reminded.

**Arrival:** Alfie wears his football kit under his coat. Lucy asks whether there is somewhere to dry muddy boots, which leads to the drying space.

**Routine:** out all day at the tournament, back muddy, boots on the drying rack, an early night. On the second evening Alfie shows a medal to anyone at reception.

**Request choices:** towels after a muddy day; use of the drying space; an extra pillow.

## F03 · George Whitlock, 68; Poppy Whitlock, 11 · Grandad and granddaughter, the railway trip

**Visit:** one night, a trip to a railway museum during the school holidays. **Room:** Twin (104), or a Double/twin as available.

**Personalities:** George is gentle, slow-moving and tells the same train fact twice. Poppy is patient with him, sharp, and keeps the tickets safe in her own small bag.

**Arrival:** Poppy does the talking at the desk while George finds his glasses. She knows the booking reference by heart.

**Routine:** a quiet evening, cards at a lounge table, George asleep in an armchair for ten minutes, Poppy reading beside him. An early, careful departure.

**Request choices:** a reading lamp that works (George reads with the lamp very close); a jug of water; a taxi confirmation for the museum.
