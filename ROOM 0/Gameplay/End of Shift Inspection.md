# End of Shift Inspection

**Decided 2026-10-24:** after a shift ends, someone comes in, checks over the hotel, and rewards the players with **coins** to spend in the [[Hotel Shop]].

## What the inspector checks (proposal)
| Check | Measured by |
|---|---|
| Guests | Average guest satisfaction ([[Guests]]) |
| Tasks | Share of [[Shift Tasks]] completed, and how fast |
| Cleanliness | Rooms and corridors cleaned, no mess left |
| Maintenance | Faults fixed (lights, doors, leaks) |
| Incidents | Incidents reported or resolved ([[Incidents]]) |

## Payout
- Grade from **D to S** turns into a coin payout, split between the players by contribution, with a small team bonus so co-op stays friendly.
- A short results screen shows the grade and coins, as a clear "shift done" moment.

## Story hook
The inspector is a character, not just a menu: see [[The Inspector]]. Who sends them, and what they are *really* checking, can feed [[Room 0#The secret]].
