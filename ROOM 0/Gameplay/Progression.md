# Progression

How players move from an ordinary first shift toward [[Room 0]].

## Canon
- Unlock **restricted areas** of the [[Ashbourne Hotel]].
- Discover **documents**.
- Piece together information about **previous guests** ([[Missing Guests]]).
- Eventually discover **how to access Room 0**.
- Finding the room is **only the beginning**.

## Open

- What gates an area: number of shifts, specific evidence, solving a puzzle?
