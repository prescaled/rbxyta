# CCTV System

**Decided 2026-10-24:** **CCTV viewing inside the hotel.** This expands the camera task in [[Shift Tasks]] into a full system.

## How it works (proposal)
- **Security room**: a bank of old CRT monitors and a control desk. One player sits down to use it.
- **8 to 12 cameras** on the first floor (lobby, reception, each corridor, lift, staff room, shop, back door).
- **Controls**: switch camera, zoom, toggle night vision, take a snapshot.
- **Look**: grainy VHS filter, scan lines, a timestamp, and short static when switching.
- **Anomaly reports**: spot something wrong on a feed, take a snapshot, and press **Report**. A correct report removes the anomaly and pays coins. A false report costs a little time. (Like "I'm on Observation Duty".)
- **Only-on-camera things**: some [[Anomalies]] are only visible on CCTV, which makes the camera player essential.
- **Guide the team**: the camera player can ping a location for teammates ("someone is behind you").
- **Night Security** class can view feeds on a handheld radio ([[Classes]]).

## Why it matters
- Gives a calm-but-tense job for players who prefer watching.
- Links straight to [[The Security Archives]] videos: the same camera angles and filter.
- Unreported anomalies grow into dangerous [[Anomalies#Predators]], so watching is protection.
