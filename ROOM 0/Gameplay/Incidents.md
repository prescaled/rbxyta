# Incidents

Strange events during a shift. They should create [[Player Experience|curiosity and uncertainty]], not just jump scares.

## Canon examples (from the [[Vision]])
- A guest **calls from a room that is supposed to be empty**.
- A security camera shows **someone standing behind a player**, though the corridor is empty.
- **Management gives instructions that contradict** what another player has discovered ([[Hotel Management]]).
- **Corridors change** overnight ([[Ashbourne Hotel]]).
- **Rooms appear on camera but not on the floor plans**.

More events, by area and night: [[Strange Events]]. Threats that hunt players: [[Anomalies]].

## Types
- **Harmless**: odd but safe. Teaches players not to run from everything.
- **Story**: leads deeper into the hotel's history and gives evidence ([[Progression]]).
- Danger level and failure states are still open ([[Open Questions]]).

## Design rules
- Every story incident must point at the truth of [[Room 0#The secret]].
- Co-op twist: give different players different information (one sees the camera, another hears the call).
- The first prototype needs only "several mysterious events" ([[Roadmap#Phase 2 — Build the first shift]]).
