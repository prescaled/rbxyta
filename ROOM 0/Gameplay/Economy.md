# Economy

How coins flow in ROOM 0.

## Earn
| Source | Size | When |
|---|---|---|
| [[End of Shift Inspection]] | Big, predictable | End of every shift |
| [[Variable Rewards]] (tips, lucky finds) | Small, random | During the shift |
| Guest group bonus ([[Guests]]) | Medium | When a group checks out happy |

## Spend
| Sink | Notes |
|---|---|
| [[Hotel Shop]] boosts | Main sink, items last one shift |
| [[Classes]] unlocks | Permanent earned-role purchases; proposed coin calibration and shift requirements in [[Classes - Core and Progression]] |
| (Later) cosmetics: uniforms, name badges, lockers | Optional, good long-term sink |

## Robux
- **Shop reroll** (decided).
- Ideas for later: cosmetics, private servers. Keep Robux away from story progress ([[Principles]]).

## Balance starting point (tune in playtests)
- One average shift should afford about **one or two** boosts.
- A great shift (S grade) should afford about **three**.
