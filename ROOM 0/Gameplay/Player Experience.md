# Player Experience

Three feelings ROOM 0 must create.

## Curiosity
Players should constantly question what they see. A small detail in a guest's room might become important several shifts later.

## Uncertainty
Not everything strange should be dangerous. Players should investigate rather than run away whenever something unusual happens. See [[Incidents#Types]].

## Discovery
Players should feel rewarded for paying attention. **A clue mentioned in a TikTok might be an actual object hidden somewhere in the game.**

## The video-to-game bridge
This is what links the [[Content Strategy]] to the game. A viewer could recognise a **room number, a name, a symbol, or a date** from a video, then find out in the game that the detail matters. That is a stronger pull than "join our game".

Keep a list of every planted clue: where it appeared in a video and where it is in the game. (Suggested: add a "Clues" table to [[Content Strategy]] once the first ones exist.)
