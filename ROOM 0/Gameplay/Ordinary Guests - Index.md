# Ordinary Guests - Index

The guests who go home: a detailed design for the ordinary people staying at the Ashbourne. Prepared at Rivin's request for shared development with Prescaled.

**Scope:** 20 proposed bookings, 46 named individuals, party sizes 1–4, six detailed chapters, 30 request types, guest-to-guest scenes, ordinary misunderstandings, return visits and normal departures.

The 1–4 group framework and ordinary-guest focus are established direction. Named characters, detailed mechanics and tuning numbers are proposals. These notes do not imply that models, animations or NPC systems have been implemented.

## Recommended starting set

Martin; Leena; Gwen and Arthur; Aisha and Tom; Nia, Beth and Callum; Dawn, Pete, Ivo and Kelly. These six bookings cover all party sizes and 13 unique people, to be introduced in stages rather than all at once.

## Key design decisions to preserve

- A booking is a room reservation with named people, not a headcount.
- Every person has a distinct identity; companions can act independently without disappearing.
- Four-person bookings require approved four-person sleeping arrangements.
- Requests have plausible causes, clear completion and reasonable fallback options.
- A content guest may ask for nothing.
- Fixed travel plans still matter when a guest is happy; extensions are conditional.
- Departures are visible and ordinary. Bills close, possessions leave and rooms are cleaned.
- The missing cases remain separate from this catalogue.
- Shared behaviours are reusable; the catalogue is not a demand for 46 custom animation systems.
