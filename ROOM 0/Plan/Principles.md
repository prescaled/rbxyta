# Principles

## The identity
> Every room has a number.
> Every guest has a record.
> Every disappearance has an explanation.
> **Except Room 0.**

## Rules we follow
1. **Not a Roblox game with some AI videos attached.** A fictional mystery told across TikTok, YouTube and Roblox.
2. **Truth first.** Decide [[Room 0#The secret]], then work backwards to clues, characters, twists and gameplay. No random mysteries.
3. **Coherent universe.** Small details must eventually connect.
4. **Small and excellent.** We are two people. Version one = one excellent gameplay loop ([[Gameplay Loop]]), one memorable location ([[Ashbourne Hotel]]), a handful of genuinely interesting mysteries.
5. **Expand only after proof** that people enjoy both the stories and the gameplay ([[Roadmap]]).
6. **Fiction, clearly.** Videos are presented as a fictional story, never as real evidence ([[Content Strategy]]).
7. **Don't depend on one viral video.** Build a world people recognise by name.
