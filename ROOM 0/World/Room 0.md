# Room 0

The central mystery of [[ROOM 0 - Home|ROOM 0]].

> What is Room 0, and why does the hotel seem determined to keep it hidden?

## What we know (canon)
- It is somewhere inside the [[Ashbourne Hotel]]. Nobody knows exactly where.
- Nobody knows why it exists.
- [[Hotel Management]] refuses to acknowledge it.
- In 2026 the hotel computer lists [[Daniel Mercer]] as still checked in, now in **ROOM 0 — OCCUPIED** ([[Timeline]]).
- Players may eventually discover how to access it ([[Progression]]). **Finding the room is only the beginning.**

## The secret
**UNDECIDED.** This is the next major creative decision.

The rule from the [[Vision]]: decide the truth first, then work backwards to create clues, characters, plot twists and gameplay that all connect. Do not invent mysteries as we go.

Candidate directions are collected in [[Open Questions#Candidate directions for the secret]]. None of them are canon.

## Requirements any answer must meet
- Explains why guests disappear and why records change (Mercer's room number).
- Explains corridors changing and rooms that exist on camera but not on plans.
- Gives management a reason to hide it.
- Can be revealed a piece at a time across videos and shifts.
- Leaves room for "finding the room is only the beginning".
