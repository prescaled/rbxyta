# Ashbourne Hotel

The fictional hotel where everything in [[ROOM 0 - Home|ROOM 0]] happens.

## On the surface
- From the outside it is an ordinary hotel.
- Guests arrive, employees work their shifts, rooms are cleaned, and everything appears normal.

## Its history (canon from the [[Vision]])
- **Guests have disappeared.** The first known case is [[Daniel Mercer]] in 1998. More cases live in [[Missing Guests]].
- **Corridors change overnight**, according to employee reports.
- **Some rooms appear on security cameras but are not on the floor plans.**
- Somewhere inside is [[Room 0]]. Nobody knows where it is or why it exists.
- [[Hotel Management]] refuses to acknowledge it.
