# Pilot: Daniel Mercer

The first video, from the [[Vision]]. Series: [[The Missing Guests]].

## Script
| Visual | Narration |
|---|---|
| Old hotel security footage | "In 1998, a guest checked into the Ashbourne Hotel. His name was Daniel Mercer. He was assigned Room 214. Three days later, the room was found empty. His belongings were still there. The security cameras showed him entering his room, but never leaving." |
| Cut to a hotel corridor | "Twenty-eight years later, a new employee discovered something strange. Daniel Mercer was still listed as a guest. His room number had changed." |
| Zoom into an old computer monitor | (no narration) Screen reads: **ROOM 0 — OCCUPIED.** |
| End | No explanation. Just a name: **ROOM 0**. |

## Goal
Make people ask: who is [[Daniel Mercer]]? What happened to him? Why does a room numbered zero exist?

## Production
- Narrator voice in ElevenLabs ([[Production Tools]]).
- Hide one detail in a frame that the narrator never mentions, and that later exists in the game.
