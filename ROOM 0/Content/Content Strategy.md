# Content Strategy

The story is told **before** the game launches, through short fictional videos on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

## Series
| Series | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| [[The Missing Guests]] | Mock documentaries about people who disappeared | Introduce characters, dates and clues that connect |
| [[The Security Archives]] | "Recovered" surveillance recordings | Hide details the narrator never mentions |
| [[The Employee Files]] | Internal documents, training videos, odd workplace rules | Introduce mechanics and rules players meet in game |
| [[The Discoveries]] | Real Roblox footage | Reveal that viewers can explore the hotel themselves |

## Rules
- Look like carefully produced fiction, **not generic AI slideshows**.
- A recognisable visual identity across every video.
- Present it as a **fictional story**, not as genuine real-world evidence.
- Every video should plant at least one detail that matters in the game ([[Player Experience#The video-to-game bridge]]).

## Rollout
Follows the [[Roadmap]]: pilots in Phase 1, more Shorts plus matching in-game discoveries in Phase 3, videos pointing to the game in Phase 4.
