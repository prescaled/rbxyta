# Hotel Management

The people running the [[Ashbourne Hotel]].

## Canon
- Refuses to acknowledge [[Room 0]].
- Gives instructions to staff that can **contradict** what players discover ([[Incidents]]).

## Design use
- Management is the voice of "official" reality. Their instructions are a gameplay mechanic: players must decide whether to follow them.
- [[The Employee Files]] videos can show their training material and odd workplace rules, which then appear in the game.

## Open
- Do they know the truth, fear it, or serve it? Depends on [[Room 0#The secret]].
- Is there a named manager character (a voice on the phone, a memo signature)?
