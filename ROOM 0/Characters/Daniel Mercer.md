# Daniel Mercer

The first known disappearance at the [[Ashbourne Hotel]], and the face of the [[Pilot - Daniel Mercer|pilot video]].

## Canon
- Checked in **1998**, assigned **Room 214**.
- Three days later the room was found empty, with his belongings still inside.
- Security cameras showed him entering his room, but never leaving.
- 28 years later (2026), the hotel system still lists him as a guest, now in **[[Room 0]] — OCCUPIED**.

## Open
- Who was he? Age, job, why he was staying at the hotel.
- What were his belongings? (Good source of clues to hide in Room 214 in the game, see [[Player Experience]].)
- How does he connect to the next missing guest? See [[Missing Guests]].
- Is he still "inside" the hotel in some way? Depends on [[Room 0#The secret]].
