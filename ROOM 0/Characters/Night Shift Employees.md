# Night Shift Employees (the players)

Players take the role of **newly hired night-shift employees** at the [[Ashbourne Hotel]].

## Canon
- Play with friends or alone.
- Do normal hotel work ([[Shift Tasks]]) while strange things happen ([[Incidents]]).
- Goal: complete the shift, uncover evidence, and gradually understand the hotel's secrets ([[Gameplay Loop]]).

## Health and boosts
- Players have **health** that scary events can lower; a First Aid Kit from the [[Hotel Shop]] recovers it.
- Players earn coins at the [[End of Shift Inspection]] and from [[Variable Rewards]].

## Open
- Roles inside a team (front desk, security, maintenance) or everyone does everything?
- Can a player be "lost" like a guest? Is there failure? See [[Open Questions]].
