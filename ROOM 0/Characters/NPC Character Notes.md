# NPC Character Notes

Shared character index for Rivin and Prescaled. Updated 27 October 2026.

## Staff alongside the new hires

**User-established direction, 27 October:** the Ashbourne has NPC employees who carry out duties alongside the players, who are new staff. Players can interact with every staff member. Some have useful insights, some are friendly without having special information, and one or two do not speak.

The detailed cast, names, ages, appearances, dialogue and scheduling below are proposals developed from that direction. They are not claims of implemented NPCs or newly settled story canon.

- [[Resident Staff]]: cast overview, purpose, story boundaries and recommended starting group.
- [[Resident Staff - Character Profiles]]: seven detailed employees, their jobs, personalities, backstories, appearance and conversation examples.
- [[Resident Staff - Interactions and Routines]]: working together, conversation states, silent interactions, schedules, guest scenes and implementation requirements.

"Resident staff" means the regular hotel workforce, not a claim that they live in hotel bedrooms. The players remain [[Night Shift Employees|newly hired night-shift employees]]. Named NPC staff do not replace [[Classes|selectable player classes]].

## Ordinary guests

[[Ordinary Guests - Index]] remains the guest catalogue. Its solo, pair, group and later family proposals are preserved. [[Ordinary Guests - Character Sheets]] contains guest appearance/build references.

Staff and guests occupy separate catalogues. An employee working reception is not a guest booking, does not consume a room automatically and does not inherit guest checkout logic.

## Missing cases and established story characters

- [[Missing Guests]]: the three missing cases.
- [[Daniel Mercer]]: the first known disappearance, in 1998.
- [[Marion Marnie Bell]]: Case 2, with disappearance year and links still open.
- [[Vincent and Audrey Shaw]]: Case 3; author-only explanation remains separate from player dialogue.
- [[Hotel Management]]: the official institutional voice, with unresolved motives.
- [[The Inspector]]: the existing end-of-shift inspection character.
- [[The New Employee]]: the earlier record discoverer; their relationship to the players remains open.

The new cast is not an excuse to answer those open questions by implication. None of the seven is silently assigned as the Case 3 waitress, the unnamed manager, the Inspector, the earlier New Employee or a witness to Mercer's disappearance.

## Scope of this update

Character and interaction design only. No models, animation rigs, Roblox scripts or collaborator-owned scene folders were changed. The files are a shared reference for future writing and implementation, subject to the existing [[Art Bible]], [[Collaboration Workflow]] and [[Work Board]].
