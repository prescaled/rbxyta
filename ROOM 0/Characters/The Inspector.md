# The Inspector

The person who arrives at the end of every shift, checks over the hotel and pays the staff ([[End of Shift Inspection]]).

## Canon (2026-10-24)
- Comes in after the shift ends.
- Checks the hotel and rewards players with coins.

## Ideas (not canon)
- Works for [[Hotel Management]], but never gives a name.
- Always wears the same clothes and always arrives at exactly the same time.
- Sometimes inspects rooms that are not on the floor plan, or writes down a guest the players never checked in.
- Could be the one character who knows where [[Room 0]] is.
